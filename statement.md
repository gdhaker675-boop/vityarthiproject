## Problem Statement

Hostel/Mess Complaint Management System

1. Introduction

Hostel and mess facilities are an important part of student life. Students often face problems related to room maintenance, cleanliness, water supply, and food quality. However, managing these complaints manually can be difficult, and students may not know whether their complaints have been resolved.

The Hostel/Mess Complaint Management System is a Python-based project designed to provide a simple and organized way to register, manage, and track complaints related to hostel and mess facilities.

2. Problem Statement

In many hostels, students report their complaints verbally or through informal communication. This can lead to difficulties in maintaining complaint records and tracking their progress.

Students may have to repeatedly ask hostel staff about the status of their complaints. There is a need for a simple system that allows students to register complaints, view complaint details, and check their current status.

This project aims to address these problems by developing a menu-driven Python application that stores complaint details, assigns a unique complaint ID, and allows complaint status to be updated.

3. Scope of the Project

The scope of this project includes:

- Registering complaints related to hostel and mess facilities.
- Collecting student names, room numbers, complaint categories, and descriptions.
- Assigning a unique ID to each complaint.
- Displaying all registered complaints.
- Checking complaint status using the complaint ID.
- Updating complaint status to Pending, In Progress, or Resolved.

The current version is a console-based application that stores complaint records temporarily in memory.

4. Target Users

1. Students: Students can register complaints and check their status.

2. Hostel Administration: Hostel staff can view complaints and update their status.

3. Mess Management Staff: Mess staff can review complaints related to food quality and other mess facilities.

5. High-Level Features

- Complaint Registration
- Hostel and Mess Complaint Categories
- Unique Complaint ID Generation
- View All Complaints
- Complaint Status Tracking
- Complaint Status Update
- Menu-Driven User Interface

6. Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to make the complaint management process more organized and easier to use. It also helps students apply Python programming concepts such as functions, loops, conditional statements, lists, and dictionaries to solve a real-world problem.

7. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a simple Python application that allows users to register complaints, view complaint records, and track their status through a unique complaint ID.
